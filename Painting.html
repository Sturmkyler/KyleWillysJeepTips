Painting

For painting your jeep in the iconic olive green color I would go with paint code 33070 from Ron Fitzpatrick jeep parts you will need around 2 gallons for painting your jeep mix your paint out with a enamel reducer then you have two options either you do it the historically correct way of doing one single coat or do multiple coats that way if it gets scratched

The orange primer doesn't stick through now for doing your Identification tags and the invasion markings get two cans of Rustoleum's 7790 Flat White Enamel and to paint your marking the stencil kit from Kaiser.

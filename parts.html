Where to Buy Parts

There are four suppliers when thinking about getting parts for your Willys or GPW Jeep: Kaiser Willys, JeepsterMan, Joe's Motor Pool, and Ron Fitzpatrick Jeep Parts. Each of these sites has its good and bad qualities.

Kaiser Willys and JeepsterMan both have similar parts catalogs. Their engine parts and body parts are made to similar standards, but their electrical supplies can sometimes be lower quality.

Joe's Motor Pool and Ron Fitzpatrick Jeep Parts are smaller suppliers, but their parts are often made to very high standards. Their gauges and wiring harnesses are typically made in the United States or the United Kingdom. They can be more expensive, but the quality can make them worth the price.

From my experience, Kaiser Willys starter switches can have problems with overheating, and some wiring harnesses can be too short. Joe's Motor Pool starter switches are built with thicker materials and have worked better for me.

I recommend getting parts from all four suppliers depending on what you need. I would use JeepsterMan for body parts, Kaiser Willys for engine parts, and Joe's Motor Pool or Ron Fitzpatrick Jeep Parts for electrical parts, gauges, and wiring.
